Words of Logic & Code

Great pioneers in computer science and mathematics have left behind powerful ideas. Here are some of our favorites.

Alan Turing

Alan Turing, the father of theoretical computer science, once said: Machines take me by surprise with great frequency.

We can only see a short distance ahead, but we can see plenty there that needs to be done.

Ada Lovelace

Ada Lovelace, considered the first computer programmer, noted that that brain of mine is something more than merely mortal.

The Analytical Engine weaves algebraic patterns just as the Jacquard loom weaves flowers and leaves.

John von Neumann

John von Neumann believed that if people do not believe that mathematics is simple, it is only because they do not realize how complicated life is.

Young man, in mathematics you don't understand things. You just get used to them.

Grace Hopper

Grace Hopper, a pioneer of computer programming, often warned that the most dangerous phrase in the language is, 'We've always done it this way.'

A ship in port is safe, but that's not what ships are built for. Sail out to sea and do new things.
